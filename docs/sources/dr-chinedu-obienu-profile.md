DR. CHINEDU OBIENU

Chinedu Obienu holds a PhD in law. His practice spans through corporate commercial, energy, taxation, and general governmental compliance in Nigeria. He has practiced law for over two (2) decades, and is steadily climbing the ladder of excellence. He is a Member- Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (MCIArb) (UK), a Fellow-Institute of Chartered Mediators and Conciliators (FICMC), He is an Alumnus of the prestigious Haggai Leadership Institute (HLE) Hawaii USA. He is an accredited Management Trainer and Consultant, verified by Nigeria Council for Management Development (NCMD). He is a Consummate Litigator and and a dispute Settlor. He is the Principal/Managing Partner of Zest Partners, a firm of Legal Practitioners based at Abuja. Chinedu Obienu is an author, and has written and presented several papers in conferences locally and internationally. He facilitates workshops, seminars and training in diverse areas of law. He is a Member of the Nigerian Bar Association and African Bar Association. He is was on Wednesday 5th August 2026, inaugurated by the Attorney General of the Federation and Minister of Justice as Member of the Governing Council of the Legal Aid Council of Nigeria (LACON)
